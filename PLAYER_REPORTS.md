# Player reports

How Weeping of the Empire runs for players, in their own words. **Everything here is self-reported by players and not
verified by us.** Good and bad reports are both listed, as written; where we changed something since, the last column
says so. Numbers depend on the scene, settings and version, so treat them as one player's experience, not a benchmark.

Something to add? Open an [issue](https://github.com/daniel-madrid-07/weeping-of-the-empire/issues/new/choose) for a
problem, or post how it runs in [Discussions](https://github.com/daniel-madrid-07/weeping-of-the-empire/discussions).

"The other port" means another PC version of the game players compared with.

Reports up to 7 November 2026 (v0.1.10 is the latest release); the "Since then" column is updated with each release.
